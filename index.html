My dearest Krethi ma,

I do not only love the version of you that smiles for photos. I love the sleepy one, the overthinking one, the one who works too hard, the one who needs silence, the one who laughs until the whole moment changes. I love the parts you show easily and the parts that still need gentleness.

You never have to perform happiness for me. You never have to be less complicated, less emotional, less yourself. If the world has ever made you feel like you were too much, let this page keep reminding you: to me, you are not too much. You are the exact amount of magic my life was missing.

Since January 14, ordinary days have carried your name. Car rides became memories. A photo became something I could stare at for far too long. A tiny message from you could change the temperature of my whole day.

I cannot promise every day will be perfect. I can promise that I will keep trying to be a safe place, that I will listen, that I will learn you again whenever life changes you, and that I will never make you earn the softness you deserve.

So come here exactly as you are, bbygurl. Bring your bright days and your heavy ones. Bring your dreams, your fears, your EY stories, your music, your silence, and every future version you are still becoming. I want to know all of them.

If love is choosing, then this is me choosing you—in the seconds above, in every hundred-day chapter, and in all the quiet days between them.

With all my heart, Naveesen 💛
